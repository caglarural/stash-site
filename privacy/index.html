Stash

Privacy Policy

Effective: October 7, 2026

Stash is designed as a local-first personal capture and rediscovery app. This policy explains how Stash handles information when you use the app.

Data we collect

Stash does not collect personal data for the developer. Stash does not use advertising, analytics, tracking, crash-reporting, account, or cloud-sync services that send your saved content to the developer.

Data stored on your device

Your saved links, notes, tags, collections, favorites, archive state, reminder settings, and related app data are stored locally on your device. Stash uses a local SQLite database. The Share Extension uses an Apple App Group container to hand a capture to the main app locally.

Network requests for links and previews

When you save or view a link, Stash may connect to the website you selected to retrieve page metadata or preview resources. Opening a link preview may also connect to that website using Apple's native browser technology. These requests go to the relevant website or resource provider, not to a Stash backend. Those third parties may receive standard network information such as your IP address and may process it under their own privacy policies.

Deepen with AI

Stash does not connect to an AI model or AI service. The “Deepen with AI” feature prepares text on your device and opens the iOS Share Sheet. Nothing is sent to another app or service unless you choose a destination and share it. Any app or service you select then handles that content under its own terms and privacy policy.

Notifications

Stash can schedule local reminders on your device. Notification permission is requested only after a relevant user action. Stash does not use a remote push-notification backend for these reminders.

Accounts, advertising, and tracking

  • No Stash account or login is required.
  • No advertising SDK is used.
  • No cross-app tracking is used.
  • No user profile is created on a Stash server.

Your control

You can delete saved items from within Stash. This removes those items from Stash's local database. iOS controls app-container removal when you uninstall an app.

Children

Stash is a general-purpose productivity app and is not specifically directed to children.

Changes to this policy

If Stash's data practices change, this policy will be updated before those changes are represented as current.
